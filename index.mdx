Engineering Growth is an 8-month self-study framework designed for senior backend engineers who want genuine depth in systems engineering and AI fluency. It combines a sequential topic syllabus, a proven four-technique learning method, weekly artifact requirements, and a structured review cadence — built around the thesis of **depth over breadth**.

## How the Framework Is Organized

Engineering Growth runs three parallel tracks at different weights each week.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Track A — Systems" icon="database" href="/roadmap/tracks">

  <Card title="Track B — AI" icon="robot" href="/roadmap/tracks">

  <Card title="Track C — Interview" icon="briefcase" href="/roadmap/tracks">

## Weekly Rhythm

<Steps>
  <Step title="Follow the week's plan">
    Each week's priorities are laid out in the [Roadmap](/roadmap/overview). Track A items run on weekday evenings; the weekend block handles longer work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Produce one artifact">
    Every week ends with one artifact — a synthesis, design answer, or case study reduction. Transcription doesn't count; transformation does.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the Sunday review">
    A 30-minute structured reflection using the [weekly review template](/workflows/weekly-review). Six questions, honest answers, no catch-up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update your reminders">
    Translate next week's plan into a fresh task list using the [reminders template](/workflows/reminders). Delete the old list — don't roll forward.
  </Step>
</Steps>
